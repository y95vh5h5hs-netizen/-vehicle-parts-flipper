How V1 uses eBay Product Research

  1. Tap Research on eBay for a part.
  2. In eBay Product Research, set the date range to the last 30 days and use Used/Pre-owned plus the most relevant automotive category/item specifics.
  3. Enter the resulting sold count, average sold price, sold-price range, and average shipping below.
  4. V1 calculates estimated net profit, ROI, and your maximum yard purchase price.
eBay says Product Research is free to sellers and provides up to three years of sales data, including actual sold prices, average price, sold-price range, average shipping, and sell-through rate for eligible searches. Access can depend on your Seller Hub/eBay account status.
V1 is a personal research calculator. eBay figures are entered from eBay Product Research; this app does not scrape or bypass eBay access controls. Carolina Pick-N-Pull prices should be refreshed against the current official price list before buying.
